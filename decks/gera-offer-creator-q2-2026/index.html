Offer Creator is a channel execution surface, not the whole promotion system.

Native value

Partners can create promotions that appear in distributor stores across BEES.

Boundary

Upstream ERPs may not know about BEES-only promotions; guardrails can cancel orders.

Action

Make lifecycle status and upstream acknowledgement visible.

The user needs to know whether a promotion was created, accepted, synchronized, and executable.

Research page 6198329781 · Gera integrations and strategic considerations
03 / Full evidence set

“Direct” describes two different commercial systems in the report.

BEES B2B distribution

Manufacturer → retailer

The retailer owns inventory and customer relationship; BEES supports wholesale execution through distributors and marketplace surfaces.

Gera / cosmetics model

Manufacturer → reseller

Independent sellers or consultants sell to end consumers, with campaigns, commissions, and personalized segmentation.

Why it matters

Do not copy the wrong abstraction

Shared promotion concepts do not imply the same actors, incentives, inventory, or margin structure.
